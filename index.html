How PakGig Works

PakGig is a freelance marketplace where you can hire Pakistani freelancers safely. Your payment is held in escrow until you approve the work.

1. Find a service

Browse gigs in web development, graphic design, digital marketing and SEO, writing and translation, video and animation, and AI and data entry.

2. Pay into escrow

Your money is held safely by PakGig while the seller works. After delivery you have 7 days to review and release the payment.

3. Approve and withdraw

Once you approve, the seller's earnings go to their PakGig wallet, and they can withdraw to a local bank account. The platform fee is a flat 8%.
