How does PakGig escrow work?
  1. Buyer pays upfront — funds are held securely by PakGig, not the seller.
  2. Seller delivers the work through the order page.
  3. Buyer reviews the delivery and releases payment, or requests a revision. If the buyer does not respond within 7 days, payment is released automatically.
  4. Once released, funds move to the seller's Available Balance (after PakGig's 8% fee) and can be withdrawn.
  5. If there's a disagreement, either party can open a dispute for PakGig support to review.
